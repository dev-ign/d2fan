# Fan walkthrough

The site keeps Jona’s existing dark/lime design and one shared music player.

1. Visit the homepage or a release link. Preview a song in La Música; the header player controls the same preview. Streaming links open available verified destinations.
2. In Stay Close, choose email and English/Español. Enter your email. Music-update permission is optional and unchecked; choose it only if you want updates. Join La Cultura saves your contact. When the real exclusive is configured you get a download and an email. If delivery is pending, the page says so. Confirm music updates using the separate email link/button.
3. Support the Journey starts at $15. Choose $5/$15/$30 or a custom $5–$1,000 amount, then Continue. Enter your email and optional permission. Continue saves contact before preparing payment. Back keeps your entries. A failed connection leaves your details available to retry.
4. The final step states the amount and purpose. Continue to Stripe opens secure hosted payment. Cards and eligible wallets are handled by Stripe. A tip supports the artist; a paid Vault item is a separate purchase. Neither is presented as a charity donation.
5. An available Vault card opens checkout for that item at its catalog price. Missing items stay coming soon. The supplied build keeps every paid item closed because licensed originals still need to be provided.
6. After payment, the site verifies server records before thanking you or offering a private download. Pending/failed/expired payments do not unlock content. Your paid access is retained even if the email provider needs a retry or you close the site before returning.
7. To return later, use the access email or **Already purchased? Recover access**. Fresh links go to the email used at checkout. An expired link can be replaced. A full refund revokes the refunded access unless another valid purchase maintains it.
8. Unsubscribe through the email link and button whenever you wish. Paid access and receipts remain available. Campaign measurement is a separate optional footer control. WhatsApp remains visibly unavailable until the official integration and phone verification are ready.

To try actual capture/payment delivery, the owner must first complete `docs/integrations/SETUP_GUIDE.md`. Local browser test screenshots use deterministic provider mocks; they are not evidence of live transactions.
